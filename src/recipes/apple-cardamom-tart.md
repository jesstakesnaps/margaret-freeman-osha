## Ingredients

**For the pastry:**
- 200g cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 300g plain flour
- 1 tbsp icing sugar
- Pinch of salt
- 3–4 tbsp ice cold water

**For the filling:**
- 5 medium apples, peeled and thinly sliced
- 80g caster sugar
- 1 tsp ground cardamom
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, dotted

## Method

### Make the pastry

Rub the cold butter into the flour with your fingertips until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs. Stir in the icing sugar and salt. Add ice cold water one tablespoon at a time, mixing gently until the dough just comes together. Do not overwork it.

Flatten into a disc, wrap, and rest in the fridge for 30 minutes.

### Prepare the filling

Toss the apple slices with the sugar, cardamom, cinnamon, and lemon juice. Set aside for 15 minutes.

### Assemble and bake

Preheat your oven to 190°C. Roll the pastry and line a 23cm tart tin. Blind bake for 12 minutes, then remove beans and bake a further 5 minutes until just golden.

Arrange the apple slices in overlapping circles. Pour over juices from the bowl. Dot with butter. Bake for 35–40 minutes until the apples are tender and the pastry is deep golden.

---

Leave to cool for at least 20 minutes before slicing. Serve with crème fraîche or vanilla ice cream.

> This tart is best the day it's made, though the pastry stays crisp for a second day if stored uncovered at room temperature.
