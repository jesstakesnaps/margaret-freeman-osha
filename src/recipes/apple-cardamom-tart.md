## Ingredients

* ½ cup fresh basil leaves, crushed
* 2 inches of fresh ginger, peeled and thinly sliced
* ½ cup of local honey
* 1 cup boiling water
* 5 lemons
* More basil for garnishing 

## Method

Add the crushed basil, sliced ginger, and honey to a small heatproof bowl. Pour one cup of boiling water into the bowl and stir until the honey dissolves. Set aside for 15 minutes, then strain the liquid, removing the basil and ginger. Pour the liquid into a two-quart pitcher. Juice four lemons (about 1 cup of lemon juice) and add it to the pitcher. Fill the pitcher ¾ full with ice. Add cold water to fill the pitcher to the brim. Give a good stir. Slice the remaining lemon into thin slices and stir them into the lemonade. Pour into glasses and garnish with a sprig of basil. 

Notes: If a bubbly drink sounds more refreshing, try sparkling water. To make this refreshing drink by the glass, simply steep the first five ingredients, then combine. Store it in a tightly covered jar in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Garnish with lemon slices and basil. Enjoy!
