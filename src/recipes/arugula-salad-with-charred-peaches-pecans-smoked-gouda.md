Summer is the perfect time to dress up a simple garden salad while taking advantage of in-season stone fruit. On my way out the door at Hunger Mountain Co-op, I picked up a copy of Delicious Living, a free publication, and found this recipe. I can't wait to try it. The combination of juicy, crunchy, peppery, smoky, and sweet has my mouth watering. I just so happened to pick up some nectarines, which should work equally as well as the peaches. Make sure your stone fruit is firm and not overripe for successful grilling.

## Candied Pecans

* ⅓ cup pecan halves
* 1 Tbsp maple syrup 
* ⅛ tsp. sweet smoked paprika 
* ¼ tsp. ground cinnamon 

## For the Salad

* 3 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for the grill
* 1 Tbsp  apple cider vinegar 
* ¼ tsp whole grain mustard 
* ½ tsp maple syrup
* ¼ tsp smoked salt 
* ¼ tsp sweet smoked paprika 
* Black pepper to taste 
* 2 firm peaches, sliced
* 5 oz baby arugula 
* 1 oz smoked Gouda, shaved with a cheese slicer or a vegetable peeler and sliced into strips

## Method

For the candied pecans, combine pecan halves, maple syrup, sweet smoked paprika, and cinnamon in a medium-sized skillet. Stir and reduce to medium -low when bubbles begin to form. Continue stirring until the pan is dry and the pecans begin to stick together, 2-3 minutes. Transfer the contents to a sheet of parchment to cool while you prepare the rest of the salad.

To make the dressing, combine 3 Tbsp olive oil, apple cider vinegar, mustard, maple syrup, smoked salt, smoked sweet paprika, and freshly ground black pepper in a small bowl or jar. Set aside. 

Heat a stovetop grill or barbecue grill over high heat. Brush with olive oil. *I will also coat each slice of fruit with oil. Grill on each side until char marks appear, approximately 2-3 minutes each side. Remove to a wire rack.

In a large bowl, toss the arugula with the dressing. Add half the peaches, half the cheese, and half the pecans, then toss gently to avoid breaking the fruit. Transfer to a serving bowl or platter and arrange the remaining peaches, pecans, and cheese on top.
