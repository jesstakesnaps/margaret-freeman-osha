The seventh in a series of dusk studies built from layered paper, fabric scraps, and oil paint — all sourced from the land around my house.

Each one is different. The light never repeats itself up on that ridge, and I've stopped trying to capture it exactly. These paintings are more about the feeling of standing there in the cold, watching the colour drain out of the sky, than they are about accurate representation.

This is the one I almost kept.
